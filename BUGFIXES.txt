                    metheorMultiV2 - Minimal Surgical Bug Fixes
================================================================================

Target: metheorMultiV2 (directly faithful to metheorMulti)

1. DESIGN PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Follow the original architecture and dataflow of metheorMulti strictly.
Avoid any artificial over-engineering (no custom streaming sliding windows,
no complex chunk batching channels). Keep modifications minimal, clear, and
focused strictly on fixing the two true bugs in upstream metheor.


2. THE TWO TRUE BUGS & SURGICAL FIXES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Fix 1] Strand & Coordinate Determination (src/readutil.rs)
- Original Bug:
  `if (r.flags() == 0) || (r.flags() == 99) || (r.flags() == 147)`
  used strict integer equality. Reads carrying composite flags (e.g. duplicate
  bit 0x400 -> Flag 1123, or supplementary/secondary bits) failed the check and
  wrongly fell into the reverse-strand branch ((abspos - 1)), shifting genomic
  coordinates by 1bp and destroying CpG concordance. In addition, Bismark's
  explicit conversion tag `XG` was ignored.
- Minimal Fix:
  1. If Bismark `XG` tag is present: "CT" -> OT (top strand), "GA" -> OB (bottom strand).
  2. Fallback: Bitwise flag queries `(!r.is_reverse() && r.is_first_in_template()) || (r.is_reverse() && r.is_last_in_template())`
     for paired-end, or `!r.is_reverse()` for single-end.


[Fix 2] CIGAR Soft-Clip & Extended Match Handling (src/tag.rs)
- Original Bug:
  When parsing CIGAR, `Cigar::SoftClip` was caught by `_ => {}`, leaving
  `used_read_len` unadvanced. This caused:
  1. The read sequence to align out-of-phase against the reference genome.
  2. The generated `XM` string to be shorter than the actual read.
  Also, CIGAR `=` (`Cigar::Equal`) and `X` (`Cigar::Diff`) were unhandled.
- Minimal Fix:
  1. Recognize `Cigar::Equal` and `Cigar::Diff` alongside `Cigar::Match`.
  2. Separate leading and trailing soft clips from the aligned portion:
     - The aligned read slice `read_seq[leading..len - trailing]` is aligned
       against the undisturbed genomic reference sequence, fully preserving
       natural CpG dinucleotide context at boundaries.
     - Soft-clipped bases are prepended and appended as '.' in the final XM string,
       ensuring XM length matches read length exactly.


3. ME ENTROPY CALCULATION (src/me.rs)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Retained the exact original `metheorMulti` accumulation logic:
  Accumulates 4-CpG patterns into `HashMap<readutil::Quartet, QuartetStat>`
  (single-threaded `compute_helper` for `-t 1`, multi-threaded `compute_helper_mt`
  for `-t > 1`).
- Preserved the full 22-column TSV format (chrom, pos1..4, me, c0..c15).
- Added a simple 3-line coordinate sort (`sort_by_key`) right before writing output,
  ensuring output rows are strictly and deterministically ordered by genomic coordinate.
